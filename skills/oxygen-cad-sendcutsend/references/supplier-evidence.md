# SendCutSend evidence checklist

Fetch current official information for the actual material and service:

- https://sendcutsend.com/guidelines/ — design/file preparation entry point.
- https://sendcutsend.com/materials/ — current material offerings and material-specific specifications.
- https://sendcutsend.com/services/ — selected cutting, bending, tapping, hardware or finishing service.

Follow applicable official links; redirects or missing pages are not passing evidence. Record final URL, access date, section, SKU/thickness/service conditions and units. If machine-readable feeds are discovered, inspect their current schema and provenance rather than assuming a stable API.

Build a scoped review table: feature/location, measured upload-file fact, rule/condition with source, result and required action. Unmeasured local flange/contact/relief and tool-access details remain unverified. An available catalog SKU does not validate the geometry or create a quote/order.
