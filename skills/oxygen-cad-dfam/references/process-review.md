# Additive process evidence

Choose process, material and machine before selecting thresholds. Cite the actual supplier design guide or material/printer datasheet and its access date. Do not maintain a universal numeric default wall/angle table in this skill.

- FDM: layer direction, actual nozzle/extrusion width, wall/bridge behavior, support access, bed/warping and load direction.
- SLA/DLP: supported walls/features, drainage and suction cups, support marks, wash/cure accessibility and resin requirements.
- SLS/MJF: powder escape and fused clearances; a support-angle screen is not the powder-process criterion.
- Metal powder bed: orientation, supports/thermal distortion, removal and post-machining; triangle measurements alone do not validate a metal process.

Start from a selected supplier's official resources, for example https://www.protolabs.com/resources/design-tips/ and https://www.materialise.com/en/industrial/3d-printing-technologies. Fetch the applicable process guide instead of assuming one linked page supports every comparison. A user-provided applicable production specification takes priority over generic advice.

Write a finding as: artifact/revision → measured field/method → applicable source condition → comparison → required change or missing evidence. Sampled normal chords can highlight a problem but cannot certify that every wall meets a minimum. Support areas rank a small orientation set; they are not a slicer's support-volume prediction.
